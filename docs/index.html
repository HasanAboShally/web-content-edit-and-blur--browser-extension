Content Edit & Blur

Edit, blur, and hide content on any webpage. Perfect for mockups, screenshots, and privacy.

Chrome Firefox Edge

Powerful Features

Edit Text

Click any text to edit it directly on the page. Use Alt+R to replace all occurrences. Double-click images to swap them.

Blur Elements

Click to blur any element. Click again for stronger blur. Perfect for hiding sensitive data in screenshots.

Hide Elements

Completely remove any element from view with a single click. Great for ads, banners, or distractions.

Redact

Blur can sometimes be reversed. Redact covers an element with a solid, irreversible block — nothing survives to be recovered.

Draw to Blur

Draw rectangles anywhere on the page to create custom blur regions. Perfect for blurring parts of images.

Annotate

Arrows, circles, a highlighter that keeps text readable, and text notes. Freehand pen, boxes and numbered step badges in Pro. Move or resize any mark. Marks vanish on reload unless you ask to keep them.

Site-Wide Rules

Apply a rule to every page on a domain, not just one URL. Set it once and it follows you around the site.

Auto-Save Changes

Your changes are saved automatically. They'll be restored when you revisit the page!

Quick Screenshot

Take a screenshot with all your edits applied. One click to capture and download.

How It Works

1

Click the Extension

Click the icon in your browser toolbar to open the floating toolbar

2

Choose a Tool

Select Edit, Blur, Hide, or Draw mode from the toolbar

3

Click or Drag

Click elements to apply effects, or drag to draw blur regions
