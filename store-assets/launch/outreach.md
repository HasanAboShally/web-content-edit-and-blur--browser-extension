# Independent coverage outreach

## Selection criteria

Contact people only when the extension directly serves their audience. Prioritize:

- product-management and UX newsletters that publish practical tool workflows
- QA and software-testing publications covering visual bug reports
- privacy educators explaining screenshots and screen sharing
- technical-writing communities producing visual documentation
- open-source and browser-extension publications

Do not buy links, send bulk mail, or imply that coverage is guaranteed. Personalize every note around one relevant article or audience need.

## Short outreach email

**Subject:** Local workflow for safer webpage screenshots

Hello [name],

Your piece on [specific article or audience problem] made me think Content Edit & Blur may be useful to your readers.

It is a free, open-source browser extension for editing a supported webpage, redacting sensitive details, adding annotations and capturing the visible viewport. The extension has no account, analytics, backend or cloud processing and does not transmit page content; the underlying website remains connected.

The clearest angle for your audience may be [redaction / product mockups / visual bug reports / screen sharing]. This practical guide gives the complete workflow without requiring an install:

[relevant guide URL]

Live demo:
https://hasanaboshally.github.io/web-content-edit-and-blur--browser-extension/#demo

Source and tests:
https://github.com/HasanAboShally/web-content-edit-and-blur--browser-extension

If it is not relevant, no reply is needed. If you test it, I would value honest criticism and can provide screenshots or technical details.

Hasan

## Follow-up policy

Send at most one follow-up after seven days. Add new information, such as a requested technical detail or an updated guide. Never send “just bumping this” messages.
