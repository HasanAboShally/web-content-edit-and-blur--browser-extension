# Product Hunt launch kit

## Product name

Content Edit & Blur

## Tagline

Edit, redact and annotate webpages before sharing

## Description

Edit supported webpages for mockups and bug reports. Redact sensitive details, annotate the result and capture the visible viewport. Free, open source and account-free for desktop Chrome, Firefox and Edge.

## Maker comment

Hello Product Hunt,

Content Edit & Blur started as a direct way to change webpage copy and blur a detail before taking a screenshot. It has grown into a focused toolkit for a complete visual communication workflow:

- edit text and replace images directly on a webpage
- blur or hide elements; blur or redact rectangular areas
- annotate with arrows, shapes, highlights, notes and numbered steps
- save page-specific or site-wide rules locally
- capture the visible viewport without transient extension controls in the image

The project has been maintained since 2020. Public figures checked September 9, 2026 show 6,000 Chrome users, 3,712 Edge users and 86 Firefox average daily users, with 28 ratings across the three stores. These are not deduplicated users.

The part I care about most is the trust model: the extension has no account, analytics, backend or cloud processing. It does not transmit page content. The underlying website remains connected and may observe DOM changes, and the source and test suite are public under the MIT license.

Everything is free. I would value feedback from people who create product mockups, documentation, bug reports or privacy-safe screen shares: which part of that workflow still takes too many steps?
