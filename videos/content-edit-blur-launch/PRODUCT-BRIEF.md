# Content Edit & Blur launch film — product brief

## Purpose

Create a professional product-launch and product-explanation film for the browser-store listings, release posts, README, and website. A viewer should understand the product without audio in under one minute, then know where and why to install it.

## Audience

- Product managers and designers making realistic mockups from an existing interface.
- QA and support teams creating clear visual bug reports.
- Educators and technical writers producing walkthroughs.
- People preparing screenshots, demos, or screen shares containing private information.

## Story

One person needs to share a real webpage without rebuilding it or exposing private details. They edit the copy, de-emphasize distractions, replace sensitive pixels with redaction, add concise visual direction, then capture a clean screenshot. The result stays local to their browser.

## Required product truths

- Edit rendered webpage text and replace page images.
- Blur is for non-sensitive visual de-emphasis.
- Redact replaces captured pixels with an opaque block and is the correct choice for sensitive information.
- Annotate with arrows, circles, boxes, highlights, text, pen, and numbered steps.
- Capture the visible viewport without transient extension controls.
- No account, analytics, telemetry, backend, cloud processing, or page-content transmission by the extension.
- Free, open source, and available for desktop Chrome, Firefox, and Edge.

## Production decisions

| Requirement | Execution decision |
|---|---|
| Professional launch energy | 47.2-second, 1920×1080, 30fps bespoke Remotion film with an energy climb and release-style finale. |
| Clear product explanation | Real product captures and large kinetic captions; no voiceover dependency. |
| Brand fidelity | Warm paper, near-black ink, vivid orange, Instrument Serif/Sans, editorial redaction-bar grammar. |
| Music | “House Vibez” by Lily J, Mixkit Stock Music Free License; 122.0019 BPM fitted grid. |
| Sound design | Traceable Mixkit SFX only; physical clicks, marker stroke, camera shutter, restrained whooshes, finale impact. |
| Accessibility | Every idea legible without sound; minimum 56px narrative captions and 32px supporting text; no rapid strobe. |
| Data safety | Capture only the repository’s fictional showcase fixture. Never capture a live user page or private browser chrome. |
| Store use | No unverifiable superlatives, rankings, or transient adoption counts. |
| Editing | Scene components expose copy/color props and one shared timeline; deliver music and music-free masters. |

## Scope

- One landscape master with licensed BGM and SFX.
- One identical music-free master retaining SFX.
- Editable Remotion source, storyboard, design spec, asset provenance, beat analysis, QA stills, and review contact sheet.
- Existing simple demo remains available as a functional walkthrough; this film becomes the primary launch/explainer asset.
